How to play

Get everyone to the Main Gate (north-east). Enemies have vision cones; the near (bright) zone spots you instantly, the far (striped) zone only notices movement and fills their meter over a few seconds. Bushes hide you unless they are right next to you. If any enemy's meter fills, or an enemy finds a body, you lose the map. Explore, find what blocks you, solve it, and new routes open — expect to go back and forth.

Left click / 1–4Select a character. Left click an enemy = takedown, a door = unlock/lockpick, an item = take, a ladder/vine/grate/hatch = use, a lever = use, a body = carry.
Right clickMove (replaces orders). Shift + right click appends a waypoint.
Wheel over waypointSet the wait time at that waypoint. Ctrl + wheel sets the start delay of the whole order queue (countdown ring).
SpacePause / resume. Orders can be given while paused — synchronise your squad.
T S B W LTakedown / Throw Stone / Smoke Bomb / Whistle / Lockpick (then click a target). Only if the character has the ability.
RToggle sneak / run. Running is fast but loud (7 m) and easier to spot.
CDrop a carried body here (drop it in a bush to hide it, or next to water to sink it). Click a bush while carrying to walk there and hide it.
H / XHold: clear all orders.
FCentre the camera on the selected character.
TabCycle the viewed layer (Sewers / Ground / Rooftops). Selecting a character snaps back to their layer.
Wheel / middle drag / arrowsZoom / pan.
F5 / F9Quicksave / quickload. Use them liberally.
F2Reveal mode (design view): no fog, every enemy and cone shown. For studying the map, not for playing.
EscCancel an armed ability, close this, or deselect.
